How it works

Anyone can open a drop: they deposit ERC-20 tokens up front (amount per claim × number of claims), and every Agent ID holder can then claim exactly one share — one per Agent ID identity, not per wallet. Claims are free apart from the network gas you pay for your own transaction; claimers are never asked to approve anything. After a drop's deadline, its creator can withdraw whatever was not claimed. AgentFair charges no fees and takes no cut.

A drop distributes whatever token its creator deposited. That token may have a market, or none at all, and AgentFair makes no statement about its value. Nothing here is a promise of returns.

This page has no backend and no accounts. It connects directly to your own wallet through your browser's standard wallet provider, and every transaction is shown to you in your wallet for your own confirmation before anything is signed. There is no QR-code or pairing-code login, and nothing is stored on any server.

Create a drop

Deposit tokens so Agent ID holders can each claim one share. Creating a drop takes two transactions, both confirmed by you in your wallet: first an ERC-20 approve for the exact total, then the createDrop call that pulls exactly that total into the contract. Nothing else is ever approved, and the approval amount is never open-ended.
